(D) PROCESS ENGINEER'S COMMENT

1. Locating and Re-setups

The part is a gear-shaft with overall length ~356 mm and max diameter Ø277.45 mm (mass 109.14 kg). Locating between centers is the primary scheme for all turning and gear-hobbing operations. This ensures concentricity of all diameters (Ø140, Ø175, Ø277.45) and axial accuracy of the gear zone.

Re-setup is required: in rough turning (Op. 4) — turn gear side first, then reverse for journal side. In finish turning (Op. 5) — journal side first (to achieve Ø140 tolerance with minimal deflection), then reverse for gear side. Dog clamp position changes at each re-setup — protect previously machined surfaces with copper/aluminium shims.

2. Steady Rest

L/D ratio ≈ 1.38 — borderline for steady rest need. For rough turning with heavy cuts (t = 3–5 mm), a follow-rest or fixed steady rest is recommended to prevent chatter and vibration, especially on the Ø140 journal zone which is the smallest diameter. During finish turning, the steady rest should be considered if surface finish Ra 1.6 cannot be achieved without it.

ASSUMPTION: Steady rest used during rough turning of Ø140 zone (Op. 4, Setup 2) and finish turning of Ø140 (Op. 5, Setup 1).

3. Critical Dimensions and Fits

  • Ø140+0.036+0.000 — this is a critical fit surface (appears to be a shaft journal for bearing or coupling). Tolerance band = 0.036 mm. Requires finish turning with fine feed (f ≤ 0.15 mm/rev) and sharp insert. Final verification with calibrated micrometer. Ra 1.6 mandatory.
  • Ø277.45-0.21 — addendum circle of the gear. Must be turned to final dimension BEFORE gear hobbing. The actual measured value is stamped on the part (Op. 9) and used as reference for gear inspection calculations.
  • Gear span measurement W = 129.76 mm — critical for gear meshing quality. Must be verified after finish hobbing with disc micrometer or span gauge over 2 teeth (for z = 14, m = 16, span over k teeth — k to be calculated: typically k = z/9 + 0.5 ≈ 2).

4. Gear Cutting Specifics

  • Module m = 16 is a coarse module — requires heavy-duty gear hobbing machine (minimum class 5К328 or equivalent for m ≤ 16).
  • Profile shift coefficient x = +0.78 — positive shift, meaning the hob must be radially offset from the standard position. Calculate radial feed setting: radial depth of cut from addendum circle to root = h = 34.24 mm (full tooth height). Hob center offset = x·m = 0.78 × 16 = 12.48 mm from nominal pitch line position.
  • Reference profile per ГОСТ 13755-68 — standard involute profile, pressure angle α = 20°.
  • Accuracy grade 8X — relatively coarse (industrial gearing). Allows single-pass finish hobbing with class B hob.
  • ASSUMPTION: Spur gear (β = 0°) — no helix angle indication found on the drawing. If the drawing actually specifies helical teeth, the hob setting angle and machine differential must be adjusted accordingly.

5. Induction Hardening (ТВЧ) of Teeth

Drawing note: "Зубья калить ТВЧ" — teeth to be induction-hardened. This is performed AFTER gear hobbing and deburring (Op. 12), because induction hardening after machining avoids distortion of the machined surfaces and eliminates the need for post-hardening grinding (which would be extremely difficult for m = 16 teeth without dedicated grinding equipment).

Caution: The exact HRC value is not fully legible on the drawing scan. The process engineer MUST verify the required hardness against the original drawing before issuing the final process card. Typical values for Сталь 45 with ТВЧ: HRC 45–55 (depending on application).

After induction hardening, inspect for:

  • Cracks (visual + MPI/dye penetrant if specified)
  • Distortion of tooth profile (if significant — may require profile correction or re-hobbing, which is unlikely for grade 8X)
  • Hardness uniformity across the gear face width

6. Keyway

The keyway (b = 25 mm) is located on the Ø50-0.62 section visible in cross-section A-A. This appears to be a second shaft step (smaller end) that is partially obscured in the main view. Note: The Ø50-0.62 dimension with the keyway may be located at the shaft end opposite to the gear. The process engineer should verify the exact location and length of the keyway against the original drawing.

ASSUMPTION: Keyway tolerance — N9 or P9 per ГОСТ 23360 (standard for shaft keyways). Not legible on the drawing scan.
